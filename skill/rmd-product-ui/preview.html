Design language specimen · v1.0

Calm surfaces. One accent. Short motion.

A working-document system: warm paper, white surfaces, near-black ink, one blue accent, a 12–28px type scale and 150/220/300ms eased motion. Elevation belongs to floating layers; content surfaces use rules, dividers and background steps.

Type scale

Every step derives from the 16px body.

Headings use weight 700 and negative tracking. Titles and group labels use 600. Body stays 400 at 1.7; labels and controls use 1.25. Tabular numerals carry every changing value.

Color

Warm neutral surfaces, one blue accent.

Color is reserved for action and state. Status colors are semantic and always pair a soft fill with a matching line and ink. The dark scheme inverts the accent: a lighter blue fill carries dark ink, while lighter blue is used as text.

Space and shape

4px unit, rounded rectangles, soft depth.

Spacing is a 4px scale with 16px block gaps and 32px section gaps. Fields and controls use 8px, buttons 10px, cards 12px and panels 14px. Pills are reserved for badges and switches; content surfaces do not carry shadows.

Motion

Short, eased, reversible, never decorative.

State changes use 150ms, structure 220ms and progress 300ms. Entrances use small scale changes, not slides. The standard curve is cubic-bezier(0.22, 0.61, 0.36, 1); there is no bounce and no infinite decoration. Loading spinners and skeletons are the only loops.

Components

Controls stay controls; panels carry the border.

Buttons, fields, switches, segmented choices, tabs, disclosures and feedback share one surface budget. A card is a background-only tile, a panel is the one bordered content container, and an overlay is the only place for elevation.

Accessibility

State is never carried by motion alone.

Focus rings stay visible, selected states change color and form, and reduced-motion settles every entrance at its resting frame. The hairline border is intentionally quiet; do not rely on it alone to identify a control in a strict WCAG 1.4.11 context.

  • Focus2px focus color, 2px offset; inset -2px on tabs and menu rows. Field focus adds a 3px halo at 16% (22% dark).
  • Hit area38px default control, 44px large or row-sized target, 44px slider hit target, 18px checkbox inside a 44px row.
  • Reduced motionDuration collapses to 0.01ms and iteration to 1; entry states settle at opacity 1, scale 1, transform none and filter none.
  • Forced colorsBorders use CanvasText/Highlight; platform marks return for checkboxes and radios; shadows and translucent backdrops are removed.
  • PrintMotion stops, shadows flatten, overlays expand, and charts keep the warm print-safe palette.
